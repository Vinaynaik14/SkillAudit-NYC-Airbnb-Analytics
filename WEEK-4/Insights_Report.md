# Week 4 - Insights Report

## Key Findings

1. **Manhattan has the highest average listing price** at **$196.88**, while the Bronx has the lowest at **$87.50**.

2. **Entire home/apt listings are the largest room-type category**, representing approximately **52.02%** of listings, followed by private rooms at **45.61%**.

3. **Average annual availability is approximately 113 days**, with listing prices and minimum-night requirements varying across neighbourhood groups.

## Recommendations

1. **Neighbourhood Pricing:** Use neighbourhood-specific pricing strategies, with hosts adjusting prices based on local market differences and demand.

2. **Room-Type Optimization:** Focus on improving pricing, amenities, and listing presentation for **entire homes/apartments and private rooms**, which represent the majority of listings.

3. **Availability Management:** Review periods of lower booking activity and consider flexible minimum-night requirements, pricing adjustments, and targeted promotions to improve listing utilization.
